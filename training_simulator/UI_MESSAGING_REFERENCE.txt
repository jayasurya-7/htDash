================================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What if a patient has no events for multiple consecutive days?
A: They will show "✓ All Events Completed!" for each day until they have
   another event. Preview will show their next event day.

Q: How far ahead does the Next Events Preview look?
A: Up to 30 days ahead, or until end of study (whichever is first).

Q: What if all remaining patients have no events until Day 50?
A: Preview will show nothing if no events are scheduled. User clicks
   "Advance Day" repeatedly to skip ahead.

Q: Can I customize the messages?
A: Yes! All text is in main_window.py methods:
   - _show_patient_complete_message()
   - _show_day_complete_message()
   Edit text/emojis as needed.

Q: What colors are used?
A: Sky-blue (#0ea5e9) for accents, dark slate for text, light background.
   Matches the professional light theme.
